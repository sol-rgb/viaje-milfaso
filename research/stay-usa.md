# USA leg — Miami + a beach. Working notes

**Group:** 5 Argentine friends, late 20s/30s, design-conscious, good food, a scene but not a party hostel.
**Shape:** 9 days from Buenos Aires. Miami 3 nights → beach 4 nights → back to Miami → home.
**Budget rule as given:** max USD 100 pp/night, 2 sharing, so a double up to ~USD 200/night is "in budget".
**Hard constraint added mid-research:** the group is exactly **five**, so every hotel needs 2–3 rooms and every rental needs 5 real beds.
**Date constraint added mid-research:** **University of Miami spring break 2027 is 6–13 March**, directly on top of the second date window.
**Research date:** September 2026. Travel: early March 2027.

---

## TL;DR

1. **Beach pick: the Florida Keys, Islamorada, bayside, in a house.** Not the Bahamas, not Aruba, not Turks & Caicos. The budget gap is not close — it is 3–4x.
2. **The USD 100 pp rule does not survive early March for five people.** The honest number is **USD 120–125 pp/night**. Everything under that is a shared dorm.
3. **Non-obvious finding:** in **Miami** (3 nights) the hotel beats the rental, because a USD 250–300 cleaning fee amortised over three nights is brutal. In the **Keys** (4 nights) the house wins decisively. Different answer per leg.
4. **Five is an expensive number.** Resort fees are charged *per room*. Three rooms = three resort fees. Parking is per *vehicle*, so one SUV for five is right.
5. Shift Miami to **2–5 March**, ahead of the spring-break wave, and put the Keys 5–9 March. Islamorada barely notices spring break; Miami Beach and Nassau absolutely do.

### Verdict paragraph

The Florida Keys, Islamorada, bayside, in a house. At USD 100 pp/night for five people in early March, the Bahamas and Aruba are not available at any level of compromise — Exuma averages roughly USD 1,040/night in its most expensive month with no budget tier, Aruba's Palm Beach averages USD 559, Grace Bay's realistic band is USD 500–850. Each also costs a return flight from MIA (USD 263–355), two half-days of travel out of nine, and a rental car you are already paying for sitting unused at the airport. Nassau is nominally cheapest but it is the one destination that prices specifically against US college spring break, and 6–13 March *is* US college spring break. The Keys cost nothing extra to reach and are the only option where five people get one roof. Be clear-eyed about the number though: even here the honest all-in floor is about **USD 105–125 pp/night** once the cleaning fee, the platform service fee and the 14.5% Monroe County lodging tax amortise over four nights. Getting genuinely under USD 100 means Key Largo rather than Islamorada, bayside rather than oceanfront, no private pool. And accept the real trade: **Keys beaches are not Caribbean beaches** — the natural shoreline is coral rock and mangrove, most "beach" is trucked-in sand, inshore water is shallow and grassy, and Bahia Honda is the single exception an hour south. The payoff here is *on* the water, not lying on it: sandbars, the only living barrier reef in the continental US, boat days. If pure beach beauty is the actual priority, Exuma or Turks & Caicos win outright and the honest advice is to raise the budget to ~USD 200 pp and stop pretending. At a hard USD 100 in spring-break week, the Keys are the only truthful answer.

**Spring-break sub-verdict:** Key West's Duval Street fills with breakers from the first week of March. Islamorada is a fishing and family island and barely registers it. That is a second, independent reason to base up-Keys and treat Key West as one long day trip.

## 3. Miami — which neighbourhood

**Recommend: Wynwood / Edgewater (if renting) or Upper South Beach / Collins Park–Mid-Beach (if hotel).**

- **Ocean Drive / South Beach below 15th St — avoid.** Spring-break epicentre in early March, loud, overpriced, tired.
- **Wynwood / Edgewater** — the actual design and food centre of gravity. Wynwood Walls, Rubell, Margulies, Design District 10 min north, PAMM 10 min south, Boia De and Mandolin nearby. Best base if you rent.
- **Upper South Beach (17th–30th) / Mid-Beach (30th–60th)** — walk to sand, north of the mess. Freehand, Esme, Circa 39, Hotel Rendale all sit here.
- **Brickell** — glassy, corporate, dull for three nights.
- **Little Havana** — great to eat in, fine to sleep in on a budget (Life House), not walkable at night.
- **Coconut Grove** — leafy and lovely, sleepy, far from your list.

### Verification findings worth flagging

- **Generator Miami no longer exists under that name.** 3120 Collins Ave now trades as **Hotel Rendale Miami Beach**. Yelp lists "Generator Miami – CLOSED"; Booking.com's `generator-miami` URL and Hotels.com now render as Hotel Rendale; the property's own marketing references "welcoming guests under our new name". The building is open — the brand is gone. Check post-rebrand reviews only.
- **Life House, Little Havana is open**, at 528 SW 9th Ave, 33 rooms in a 1920 mansion. Life House entered a joint venture with Lark Hotels (Dec 2024, now Lark Hospitality) but **continues to operate under the Life House brand** — it was not absorbed. Two recurring complaints in reviews: mould/mildew smell in some rooms and hallways, and the rooftop restaurant becoming a club with loud music until 2am.
- **Postcard Inn at Holiday Isle is now Three Waters Resort & Marina** (Islamorada Resort Collection). Same MM 84 site, same Rum Runner heritage, new name.
- **La Siesta Resort & Villas was closed through October** for guest-room and pool renovations — it should be freshly renovated by March 2027, but confirm reopening before booking.

### The resort fee trap (this is what wrecks the budget)

Fees are charged **per room per night**, which is why five people is so much worse than four.

| Hotel | Verified fee | Parking |
|---|---|---|
| The Standard Spa | **USD 55 + tax**/room/night (some channels list USD 62.70) | valet |
| the goodtime hotel | **USD 45.60**/room/night | **USD 50** nightly valet |
| Esme Miami Beach | "amenities fee" — guests report it added **~33%** to a 3-night bill | valet |

Plus ~13% Miami-Dade lodging tax on top. So an advertised USD 200 room is really ~USD 285 before tax, ~USD 320 after — **USD 160 pp, not USD 100**. Three rooms means three fees.

**Parking is per vehicle, not per room.** One rental SUV for five = one charge. Miami Beach valet USD 40–55/night; municipal garages USD 2–4/hour; a rental house with a driveway erases the line entirely.

### ⚠️ Miami Beach rental legality

Miami Beach **bans short-term rentals under 180 days in all single-family residential zones**. Fines start at **USD 20,000** and double with each offence; unlicensed operation in a residential zone can exceed USD 100,000. Legal zones only: the South Beach Entertainment District, North Beach Town Center, and RM-2 / RM-3. Hosts need four separate credentials (Florida DBPR licence, Miami-Dade Certificate of Use, Miami Beach BTR, Resort Tax Certificate). **A cheap-looking Miami Beach house is almost certainly an illegal listing that can be cancelled on you.**

## 7. The Keys — Islamorada

**Base: Islamorada, MM 74–90 on US-1.** ~1h45 from Miami with no traffic; allow 2h30 leaving on a Friday.

Why Islamorada over Key West:
- No travel day lost.
- Marina and charter infrastructure for the boat day.
- The only real cluster of design-minded places in the Keys (Casa Morada, Amara Cay, Morada Bay).
- **No Duval Street spring-break scene** — decisive given the 6–13 March overlap.
- **No short-term-rental ban.**

### ⚠️ Key West rental warning — do not book a house there

The City of Key West **prohibits rentals under 30 days without a transient licence**. Licences are **capped in number** and tied to specific transient-zoned properties; most residential zones prohibit STRs outright; enforcement is aggressive with high penalties. Non-transient residential property "may not be rented for less than 29 days at a time". A cheap-looking Key West listing is frequently illegal and can be cancelled on you. Islamorada and Key Largo have no equivalent ban.

### Keys hotels — blunt: none of them work for five

Islamorada averages **~USD 557/night in March** (against USD 234 in September). Peak Dec–Apr rates run **40–60% above shoulder** with **85%+ occupancy at waterfront properties Jan–Mar**.

| Amara Cay Resort | MM 80 oceanside | 2 × double-queen rooms | USD 850 | 170 |

**Cheapest honest hotel answer: La Siesta 2-bedroom villa at USD 137 pp** — the only single-roof hotel option for five, and villas were renovated through late 2026. Everything else is USD 170–196 pp. **There is no in-budget hotel in Islamorada in early March. Full stop.**

## 8. Keys — activities, day by day

**Day 4 — drive down, tarpon at Robbie's**
- 1h45–2h30 on US-1. Stop at Alabama Jack's on the Card Sound approach, or **Key Largo Fisheries** (MM 99).
- **Robbie's of Islamorada**, MM 77.5 — hand-feed 2m tarpon off the dock. **Dock access USD 2.50 pp, bait bucket USD 5.** Five minutes long, everyone's favourite photo.
- Sunset at **Morada Bay** or Lorelei.

**Day 5 — the boat day (book this first)**
- Captained charter out of Islamorada, 4–6h: **Islamorada sandbar at low tide** (waist-deep flats, anchor up, cooler, music), then snorkel **Alligator Reef Lighthouse** or **Cheeca Rocks**.
- Expect **USD 600–900 for a private half-day boat for six** in peak season = **USD 120–180 pp**. This is the one splurge worth breaking the rule for.
- Cheaper alternatives at Robbie's: group snorkel twice daily (10:00, 13:30); party-boat fishing **USD 100 pp morning / USD 75 pp afternoon / USD 100 pp night**.

**Day 6 — Key West day trip**
- 2h each way down the Seven Mile Bridge.
- Stop at **Bahia Honda State Park**, MM 37, **USD 8 per vehicle** — the only genuinely beautiful natural beach in the Keys, and worth the stop on its own merits.
- Key West: Hemingway House, Cuban coffee at Cuban Coffee Queen, the Southernmost Point queue, then the **free Mallory Square sunset celebration** (buskers, starts ~90 min before sunset, about 18:30 in early March).
- **Duval will be full of spring breakers** — drink at the **Green Parrot** instead.
- 12h door to door. Nominate a driver; DUI enforcement on US-1 is heavy.

**Day 7 — reef and ruins**
- **John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park**, Key Largo MM 102.5, 40 min north. Park's own 2.5h guided snorkel boat **USD 45 pp**; private operators from **USD 50 pp**; in-water guide ~USD 120 + USD 70 per extra snorkeller. Christ of the Abyss statue, shallow patch reefs.
- **History of Diving Museum**, MM 83, on the way back — small, odd, genuinely good.
- Late afternoon: **kayak Indian Key**. Free kayaks/SUPs come with most Islamorada resorts and many rental houses. Paddle from Indian Key Fill (MM 79) to **Indian Key Historic State Park** — ruins of an 1830s wrecking town. Calm bayside water, works even on a windy day.
- Last sunset and dinner at **Morada Bay**, feet in the sand.

**Only if you split to Key West — Dry Tortugas**
- **Yankee Freedom III**: **USD 250 per adult**, USD 240 students/seniors/military, USD 195 children 4–16. Includes park entry, snorkel gear, breakfast and lunch.
- Departs **07:30**, returns **17:30** — so it only works with a Key West overnight, not from Islamorada.
- Fort Jefferson, 110 km out in the Gulf. The single best thing in the Florida Keys and comfortably the most expensive. **Sells out weeks ahead in March; in spring-break week book by January.**
- There is a standing "$50 off with minimum two tickets" promo but it was advertised as running only through 31 Oct 2026 — do not count on it for 2027.

## 9. Getting around

**Miami needs a car. This is not optional for the itinerary above.** Wynwood → Design District → PAMM → Vizcaya → Little Havana → Key Biscayne is a 40 km loop with effectively no public transport between the nodes (the free Metromover only covers Downtown and Brickell).

- **Uber/Lyft:** at five people every trip is an XL. MIA → South Beach ~USD 35–50. A day of neighbourhood-hopping is USD 80–120 for the group. Two such days pay for a rental day.
- **Rental:** full-size SUV from MIA in early March ~**USD 70–100/day** + ~USD 20/day taxes and fees. Estimate — not a live quote.
- **Parking is the hidden cost, and it is per vehicle not per room.** One car for five is right. Miami Beach valet USD 40–55/night (the goodtime charges USD 50); municipal garages USD 2–4/hour; South Beach street parking needs the PayByPhone app; a rental house with a driveway = USD 0.
- **Argentine licences** are accepted in Florida for short visits, but rental companies may ask for an **International Driving Permit** alongside. Every driver must be named on the contract. **Under-25 drivers pay a young-driver surcharge** — with a late-20s/30s group, check nobody is still 24.
- **Keys:** the car is mandatory, US-1 is the only road, no alternative route exists. Southbound Friday afternoons and northbound Sunday afternoons are genuinely bad — add an hour. Mile markers descend 106 (Key Largo) → 0 (Key West); everything is addressed by MM. With a canal house you can walk to nothing, so someone is always driving.
- **Keep the same rental for all nine days** rather than returning and re-renting. Short re-rentals in March price badly.

## 10. March weather reality

**Miami:** the best weather Miami gets. Average highs rise **78°F → 81°F (26–27°C)** across the month, average low **70°F (21°C)**, sea **~76°F (24°C)** — swimmable but not warm for people used to it. March is statistically the **driest month**: ~6 rainy days, ~53 mm total, sky clear or partly cloudy **68% of daylight hours**.

**Keys:** 1–2°C warmer — highs ~27–28°C, lows 21–22°C, water ~24°C, dry season, minimal rain.

**The variable that actually matters is WIND, not rain.** Late-winter cold fronts sweep down the peninsula every 5–10 days bringing **15–25 kt northerlies for two or three days**, which chops up the ocean side, kills reef visibility and gets snorkel and charter trips cancelled.

Practical rules:
1. Book the boat day for the **calmest forecast day** of your four, with a spare day behind it.
2. Hold the **bayside kayak plan** as the wind-proof backup.
3. Bring **long sleeves** — an evening on a boat in a March front is genuinely cold.
