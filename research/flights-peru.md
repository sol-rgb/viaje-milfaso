# Vuelo a Perú — research notes (Buenos Aires → Lima, Feb/Mar 2027)

Research date: **20 September 2026**. Travel: **Feb–Mar 2027**, ~5 pax, economy, round trip, USD.

> **Honesty statement up front.** No booking engine will return a firm quote for late Feb / March 2027 today — most carriers on this market load schedules only ~330 days out and JetSMART's EZE–LIM NS27 schedule is not even filed yet. Everything marked `is_estimate: true` in the JSON is a reasoned estimate built from (a) published month-level fare data for the same route, (b) the airlines' own 2027 fare calendars where they already publish them (Sky and JetSMART both do), and (c) known seasonality. Flight numbers, times, airports and frequencies are **not** estimates — those come from current schedule data and are cited below. I have not invented a single flight number or route.

---

## 1. The headline findings

1. **Four airlines fly Buenos Aires–Lima nonstop, but not all from the same airport.** LATAM, Sky and JetSMART fly **EZE**–LIM. Aerolíneas Argentinas, LATAM, Sky and JetSMART fly **AEP**–LIM. Aerolíneas Argentinas does **not** have an EZE–LIM nonstop at all — it serves Lima only from Aeroparque.
2. **AEP has real, useful international service to Lima.** 18 weekly AEP→LIM, 22 weekly LIM→AEP, four carriers. This is the opposite of the usual "Aeroparque is only Brazil/Uruguay/Chile/Paraguay" assumption. See §4.
3. **One-stops barely save anything any more.** Because JetSMART and Sky now fly the nonstop at LCC prices, connecting via Santiago saves only **USD 25–35 per person** for 4–6 extra hours each way. The classic "connect through SCL/VVI to save" play is effectively dead on this pair.
4. **W2 (6–13 March) is the cheapest window**, by a wide margin over W1. Sky's own booking engine publishes a **Feb-2027 lead-in of USD 396 RT vs a March-2027 lead-in of USD 220 RT** on BUE–LIM. That single data point is the clearest evidence for the whole seasonality story.
5. **Paracas cannot be flown to.** Pisco (PIO) has no scheduled commercial passenger service. It's a road trip.
6. **Date trap:** Easter Sunday 2027 = **28 March**, so Semana Santa is 21–28 March. W3's optional **Sun 21 Mar** return is Palm Sunday. Return Sat 20 Mar.

## 3. One-stop options — routings and why they mostly don't pay

**Santiago (SCL) is the layover for ~97% of one-stop BUE–LIM itineraries.** EZE–SCL is 2h15, SCL–LIM is 3h45, and SCL–LIM has ~43 weekly flights across LATAM, Sky and JetSMART, so connections are easy. Total door-to-door 8h30m–11h depending on layover.

| Via | Carriers | Total time | Reality check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SCL** Santiago | JetSMART, Sky, LATAM | 8h30m–11h | The only one worth quoting. ~USD 25–35 cheaper than nonstop. JetSMART sometimes sells the two legs as separate PNRs — **self-transfer risk**, you clear Chilean immigration and re-check bags. |
| **VVI** Santa Cruz | Boliviana de Aviación (OB) | 10h–16h | BoA went **daily** VVI–LIM in mid-2026. Historically the Argentine bargain play, but layovers run 5–10h and on-time performance is the worst in the set. Now that JetSMART flies the nonstop, it no longer undercuts. Skip. |
| **BOG** Bogotá | Avianca | 13h–16h | Kayak logs BOG-layover itineraries averaging ~USD 393 RT — i.e. *more* than a March nonstop. Geographically absurd (BOG is 2,000 km north of LIM). |
| **GRU** São Paulo | LATAM, GOL | 12h–15h | Eastward detour. Almost never price-competitive on this pair. |
| **PTY** Panamá | Copa | 15h+ | Ignore. |

**AEP one-stops exist too** — AEP–SCL is a dense JetSMART/Sky/LATAM route and AEP–BOG is served by Avianca — so you can build AEP–SCL–LIM at roughly the same price as the EZE version.

Kayak's own comparison for this market: nonstop averages ~USD 414 RT, the BOG-layover average is ~USD 393 (−USD 21), and layovers via Santiago average roughly USD 42 below nonstop. That's the honest size of the prize.

**Verdict: book nonstop.** For five people with bags, USD 125–175 of total group saving does not buy 8–12 aggregate extra hours of transit and a missed-connection risk.

## 4. Does AEP have usable international service to Lima? — YES

This deserves its own section because the received wisdom is wrong.

- Since Argentina liberalised Aeroparque for regional international traffic, carriers may fly AEP to **Mercosur cities plus Peru and Colombia**. Peru is explicitly inside the permitted ring.
- Current schedule data shows **18 weekly AEP→LIM** and **22 weekly LIM→AEP** — about 3 flights a day each way, on **four** carriers (AR, LATAM, JetSMART, Sky).
- **Aerolíneas Argentinas serves Lima only from AEP.** There is no AR EZE–LIM nonstop in the current schedule.
- Independent corroboration: (a) flightconnections AEP–LIM and LIM–AEP schedule tables with flight numbers and times; (b) Sky Airline's and JetSMART's own booking pages both list **AEP** as a Buenos Aires origin for Lima; (c) an Argentine fare-deal site published **AR direct AEP–Lima from USD 359 RT** in May 2026; (d) a first-hand March 2026 trip report flying Lima→**Aeroparque** nonstop on Aerolíneas.
- **Caveat on sourcing:** the English Wikipedia "Aeroparque Jorge Newbery" airlines-and-destinations table does **not** list Lima. That table carries an "incomplete" banner and is stale. I'm going with the four independent live-schedule and airline-own-site sources over it — but if you want certainty, pull up aerolineas.com.ar and pick a March 2027 date; the airport code will show on the search result.

**Practical read for the group:** from Palermo/Belgrano/Colegiales, AEP is ~10–15 min. Ezeiza is 50–70 min and ~USD 30–40 by remis each way, times five people, both directions. A USD 10–20 higher AEP fare is still the cheaper door-to-door trip, and you get the Friday-night departure (LA 2368, 20:05) that Ezeiza doesn't offer. **Watch for "BUE" on OTAs** — some only reveal which airport at checkout.

### How the per-window numbers were derived

The price curve on this market is driven by **Argentine outbound demand**, not Peruvian inbound. January/December are peak; the market breaks the moment Argentine schools go back at the start of March; June is the annual floor. Sky's own USD 396 → USD 220 step between February and March 2027 is that cliff, published by the airline itself.

- **W1 (26/27 Feb → 6/7 Mar):** outbound sits in the tail of the summer peak. I scaled the LCC floor up ~20–25% from the March level. Cheapest nonstop **~USD 335** (JetSMART), cheapest one-stop **~USD 300** (JetSMART via SCL).
- **W2 (5/6 Mar → 13 Mar):** fully inside the March trough. Sky's USD 220 lead-in is achievable on midweek dates; a Sat-out/Sat-back weekend pattern books ~20–25% above lead-in. Cheapest nonstop **~USD 270**, cheapest one-stop **~USD 245**.
- **W3 (12/13 Mar → 20/21 Mar):** still in the trough but the Semana Santa run-up lifts the back end. Cheapest nonstop **~USD 285**, cheapest one-stop **~USD 255**. Add USD 30–60 pp if you return Sun 21 Mar (Palm Sunday).

Full carrier-by-carrier estimates are in the JSON. Rough shape: JetSMART cheapest, Sky +USD 15–25, Aerolíneas +USD 80–90, LATAM most expensive (+USD 110–130) but with the most frequency and a proper carry-on/bag allowance. **Compare like for like:** add ~USD 60–70 RT for a checked bag on JetSMART/Sky before declaring them the winner.

**Group-of-5 note:** cheap fare buckets on LCCs hold small inventory. Five seats at the lead-in price is exactly the thing that isn't there when you look late. Book by **late October 2026** (≈20 weeks out) and book all five in one transaction.

- **JetSMART Perú does not serve Talara or Tumbes.** It only serves Piura in the north. So on TYL/TBP it's a LATAM-vs-Sky duopoly.
- Kayak hard numbers: LIM–TYL typical RT USD 121–210, cheapest seen USD 97, cheapest one-way USD 54; **March is the cheapest month (~USD 130 avg), February the dearest**. LIM–TBP typical RT USD 121–203, avg USD 154, cheapest seen USD 84; February most expensive with a ~50% swing vs the annual low.
- That February effect means the **W1 internal flight is ~USD 20 more** than in W2/W3 — the same seasonality as the international leg.
- **Pick Talara** if you want the shortest transfer, **Tumbes** if fares or timings are better on your dates. Piura is the escape hatch if both spike; for 5 people the ~USD 30/pax fare saving (USD 150 total) roughly cancels against the cost of a longer private van, so it's a wash unless the fare gap is bigger than that.

### For Paracas — there is no flight

**LIM–PIO does not exist as a scheduled commercial route.** Pisco airport has had no regular airline passenger service since before COVID; current activity is Nazca Lines sightseeing overflights and occasional charter. Flight data shows essentially one non-Lima route on the board. This is not a pricing question — the flight does not exist, and at ~245 km nobody would fly it anyway. **Paracas = road transfer from Lima.**

## 8. Seasonality: two different clocks

**Airfare clock (Argentine outbound).** Jan/Dec peak (Kayak: Jan avg USD 520–529 RT, Dec USD 460). Market breaks at the start of March when Argentine schools go back. June is the floor (USD 352–360). Sky's own engine: **Feb 2027 USD 396 → Mar 2027 USD 220**. Moving W1's departure one week later is worth roughly **USD 60–70 per person, USD 300–350 for the group**.

**Weather clock (Peruvian coastal summer).**
- **North coast / Máncora:** at its best. **March is statistically the hottest month** (avg ~27 °C, highs to 31 °C) with the **warmest sea of the year (~25 °C mid-March)**. This makes **W3 the best beach window** of the three even though it isn't the cheapest. Caveat: March is also the **wettest** month up there (~35 mm over ~5 days) and an El Niño year would make that materially worse — worth a check closer to the date.
- **Lima:** end of summer. Warm (25–28 °C), humid, hazy. The *garúa* fog season starts in April, so late Feb/March is the last good stretch for Lima's beaches, rooftops and Barranco.
- **Andes (Cusco / Machu Picchu):** this is the **wet** season and low season. Only relevant if anyone wants to bolt on a Cusco leg — cheap, green, and rainy, with the Inca Trail closed for all of February.

**Peru inbound pricing** (hotels, tours) is in its low/shoulder band Dec–Mar, so land costs in Lima and Paracas are soft. **Máncora is the exception** — it's peak Peruvian beach season Dec–March, so north-coast accommodation is the one thing that is *not* cheap in these windows. Book Máncora beds early, especially for W3.

**Date trap:** Easter Sunday 2027 is **28 March** → Semana Santa 21–28 March. W3's optional **Sun 21 Mar return is Palm Sunday**. Return **Sat 20 Mar**.

## 9. Recommendation

**W2 — Sat 6 Mar → Sat 13 Mar 2027, nonstop, JetSMART or Sky from Aeroparque.**

- International: ~USD 270–295 pp RT nonstop (JA 7801 out / JA 7800 back, or H2 5810 / H2 5811)
- Internal: LIM–TBP or LIM–TYL ~USD 130 pp RT
- **Air total ≈ USD 400–425 pp ≈ USD 2,000–2,125 for the group of 5**
- Add ~USD 60–70 pp for checked bags if flying JetSMART/Sky
- Ground: one van Talara/Tumbes → Máncora, ~USD 55–70 for the group each way

**If beach weather beats price:** take W3 (mid-March is the warmest sea of the year in Máncora) and return Sat 20 Mar, for about USD 15–20 pp more.

**Avoid W1** unless the dates are fixed for other reasons — it costs roughly USD 65 pp more for the same trip, and the Fri-26-Feb-night departure only exists out of Aeroparque anyway.

**Book by late October 2026** and book all five seats in one transaction.

## 11. Things I could not verify, and what to check before booking

1. **JetSMART EZE–LIM flight numbers and times for Feb/Mar 2027** — the NS27 schedule isn't filed. The route and frequency (daily from 01JAN27) are confirmed by the AeroRoutes NW26 filing; the numbers are not. I left them blank rather than guess.
2. **Whether JetSMART runs both EZE–LIM *and* AEP–LIM concurrently in NS27**, or consolidates on one. Both currently appear in schedule data (JA 7801/7800 on AEP). Worth re-checking in December 2026.
3. **Exact AR frequency in March 2027** — current data shows AR1364 as daily from 1 March, but AR's Lima frequency has moved between 4x and 7x weekly seasonally.
4. **All fares.** No live 2027 quote was obtainable for specific dates. Set a Google Flights price alert on **EZE+AEP → LIM** for all three date pairs now; you'll see real numbers the moment the schedules load (roughly 330 days out, so late March / early April 2026 for W1 — already open, worth checking immediately).
5. **The Wikipedia/AEP contradiction** in §4 — resolve it in thirty seconds by running a March 2027 search on aerolineas.com.ar and looking at the airport code on the result.
